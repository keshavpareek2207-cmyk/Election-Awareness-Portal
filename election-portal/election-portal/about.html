About this portal

Matdaan Mitra ("voting friend") is a frontend-only awareness project. It explains in simple language how elections work in India and how every eligible citizen can take part.

Why your vote matters

India is the world's largest democracy. Every election decides who makes laws and spends public money, from your village or ward to Parliament. A vote is the one thing every citizen has equally, and some seats have been won by very small margins.

Key facts

  • The Election Commission of India (ECI) was set up on 25 January 1950. That day is celebrated as National Voters' Day.
  • The first general election was held in 1951–52.
  • The 61st Amendment (1988) lowered the voting age from 21 to 18.
  • NOTA (None of the Above) was introduced on voting machines after a 2013 Supreme Court order.
  • EVMs are standalone machines with no internet connection. VVPAT shows a paper slip of your choice for about 7 seconds.
  • Voter Helpline number: 1950.
